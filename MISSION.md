# Pitch Cruzer — Product Mission

*Written by Cruz, 2026-10-17. This is the guideline for everything in this project — product decisions, copy, feature priorities, and how the app talks about itself.*

---

You practice anywhere, your room. Your car. Your shower. Your stage with Your music. real music, obscure cult classics, tv and cinematic deep cuts, or kid rock and queen B. It’s Your voice and it can take center stage or share it with anyone from prince to the king of pop. And most importantly it’s Real feedback.

Created by a music lover who understands what it means to be better seen and not heard, and knows becoming a better singer takes more than just singing along. Pitch Cruzer turns the songs you love into opportunities to develop your voice with the proper approach to sing any song your way.

Choose a song you want to sing. Pitch Cruzer separates the original vocals from the music, analyzes the artist’s pitch and timing, and listens as you perform alongside or follow the leader style until you’re ready to say bye bye bye and strike out on your solo career. It shows you where you’re on target, where you’re falling short, and gives you the tools to improve.

No musical background required. No auditions needed. No audience necessary. No pressure to perform for anyone but yourself.

Whether you’re discovering your voice for the first time, overcoming stage fright, or sharpening skills you’ve spent years developing, Pitch Cruzer meets you where you are and helps you hear how far you’ve come.

Practice privately. Improve measurably. Build confidence one song at a time.

Because you shouldn’t have to be confident enough to sing in front of people before you have somewhere to learn.
